## Rules that keep this small

- A unit is one focused sitting, usually 30–60 minutes.
- `complete` means you have evidence: a worked exercise, notebook, written explanation, or project decision.
- `in-progress` means the unit is the current thread. There should normally be one.
- Capture the study session in today's daily note; it does not need to be polished.
