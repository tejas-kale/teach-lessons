ComponentFootball specification
EligibilityTop-flight clubs after matchweek 10, coach in post for ≥8 matches, no announced departure.
StrategiesDismiss within 48 hours; or retain for at least the next five matches.

OutcomeTotal league points over five subsequent matches.
EstimandFive-match per-protocol effect among eligible clubs.
AnalysisClone eligible club-weeks, censor on strategy deviation, weight for censoring.

Why alignment matters

If “treated” clubs enter only when dismissal occurs but retained clubs enter at matchweek 10, treated clubs must survive dismissal-free until their later entry. That immortal time is built into group definition. Align eligibility, strategy assignment and follow-up at the same time zero.
