Adjustment · 16 minutes

Three routes to an adjusted effect

Your win: explain what weighting, standardisation and augmented weighting each reconstruct.

Paper anchor. Bryson et al. used entropy balancing on pre-departure covariates, then weighted outcome regression, to study head-coach turnover in four European countries.
Weighting
Rebuild a pseudo-population in which observed confounders are balanced across treatment.
Standardisation
Predict outcomes under each treatment, then average both predictions over one target covariate distribution.
AIPW
Combine an outcome model with inverse-probability residual correction.

ATE_AIPW = mean[m₁(X) − m₀(X) + A(Y−m₁(X))/e(X) − (1−A)(Y−m₀(X))/(1−e(X))]

Worked reasoning

Entropy balancing makes control spells resemble turnover spells on chosen moments of recent form, rank and expectations. Weighted outcome regression then compares points. AIPW adds two fitted outcome surfaces and propensity-based residual corrections. It is “doubly robust” in the specific sense that consistency can survive misspecification of one nuisance model, not arbitrary confounding, non-overlap or two bad models.

Evidence-gap note. No strong standalone football AIPW paper was located. The AIPW calculation is a constructed extension of the coach-turnover setting, not a method claimed by Bryson et al.
