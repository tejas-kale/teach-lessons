Construct the counterfactual trajectory

Your win: evaluate a synthetic control through pre-treatment fit and placebo gaps.

Papers. Breidenbach & Mitze built separate synthetic controls for 41 football-hosting districts. Kleven, Landais & Saez constructed synthetic-country comparisons around footballer tax reforms.

Synthetic outcome at t = Σⱼ wⱼYⱼₜ, where wⱼ≥0 and Σⱼwⱼ=1

The design recipe

  1. Define the treated unit, intervention date and donor pool before looking at effects.
  2. Choose weights that reproduce pre-treatment outcomes and predictors.
  3. Plot the treated-minus-synthetic gap over the full period.
  4. Reassign treatment to donor units to form placebo gaps.
  5. Compare post/pre fit ratios, excluding placebo units with hopeless pre-fit transparently.

Worked reasoning

If a hosting district’s infections are badly matched before matches resume, a large post-treatment gap is unpersuasive. Excellent pre-fit makes the extrapolation more credible, but spillovers into neighbouring donor districts can contaminate the synthetic control.

Synthetic control plus DiD compares post-reform deviations after constructing a better comparison trajectory. It does not make parallel trends automatic; it makes their pre-treatment plausibility visible.
