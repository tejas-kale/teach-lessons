Do not call regression to the mean a coach effect

Your win: explain what fixed effects remove and why a pre-dismissal slump still matters.

Paper. De Paola & Scoppa compared team-season fixed-effects estimates with matching estimates for Serie A coach dismissals.

Pointsᵢₜ = αᵢ,season + λₜ + β Dismissalᵢₜ + γXᵢₜ + εᵢₜ

Team-season fixed effects absorb stable features within a team-season — squad quality, broad ambitions and persistent resources. Time effects absorb shocks common to teams. Identification comes from within-team-season variation.

The Ashenfelter dip

Dismissal often follows an unusually poor run. Even with no treatment effect, performance may revert towards its usual level. A naïve before/after comparison mistakes recovery from an extreme negative shock for improvement caused by the new coach. The paper found naïve gains that largely vanished under stronger comparisons.

What fixed effects do not solve

  • Time-varying shocks that trigger dismissal and predict future points remain threats.
  • Opponent strength and schedule composition need explicit handling.
  • Anticipation can blur the treatment date.
  • Standard errors should respect repeated observations within teams.
