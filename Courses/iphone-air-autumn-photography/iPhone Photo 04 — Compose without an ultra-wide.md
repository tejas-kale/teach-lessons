26 mm from standing eye-height is a document of pavement. You need a foreground, a height change, or 2×.

≈8 minutes · Week 2 walk

Phone landscapes fail because the Fusion Main sees a lot of ground. Pro tutorials say “put a rock in the ultra-wide.” You do not have that lens. You have feet, a grid, a level, and an optical-quality 2× crop.[¹](#s1)

If you cannot name the picture in five words — “maple against yellow Plattenbau” — you have not framed yet.

## Tools that are actually on this phone

- **Foreground:** a leaf on cobble, a railing, a bicycle wheel, a puddle. Lower third for the near thing; upper third for the tree or façade. The grid exists for this.[²](#s2)
- **Frame in a frame:** Hinterhof arch, tram shelter, gap between vans. 1× loves this; 2× often kills the frame.
- **Verticals:** Berlin façades punish tilt. Use the level. Remaining keystone: Photos → Crop → perspective sliders, *lightly*.
- **Leading lines:** canal edge, tram track, wet kerb. Start the line in a corner, not dead centre, unless the symmetry is the joke.
- **Height:** phone near the leaf litter, or up a step / bridge / U-Bahn exit. Eye-height is the default, not a composition.
- **Panorama** when you cannot step back — hips, not arms. Still not an ultra-wide walkaround lens.

Move your feet instead of zooming. 2× is a compositional lens, not binoculars. Past 2× is scouting.

## Check yourself

The frame is “nice street” with no subject. What is the first honest fix?

- [ ] Pinch toward 5× until the tree fills more of the frame.
- [ ] Switch on Bright style so the foliage starts to pop.
- [ ] Shoot a 48MP file and crop the pavement out later.
- [ ] Kneel, find a foreground, or step in and use 2×.

> [!tip]- Reveal the answer
> Digital zoom, Bright, and a 48MP rescue crop all postpone the decision. The Air’s honest tools are height, a near object, or the 52 mm crop — then walk.

> [!abstract] Assignment 2 · twenty minutes, twelve ideas
> A loop you already walk. Twelve frames, twelve different ideas, not twelve nearly identical trees: Rule of thirds · dead-centre symmetry · low · high · through something · reflection · detail that is not a leaf · a person-scale figure or bicycle · tight 2× · deliberately empty · colour against grey · panorama of a space you cannot step back from. Cull to four. Edit as in Lesson 3, plus crop only when the composition is about 10% off — do not crop a confused picture into a good one. Publish: four images, each with one sentence naming the compositional idea.

## Retrieval Practice

Why does 26 mm from standing height so often fail as a “landscape,” in one sentence?

> [!example]- Model answer
> It includes too much pavement and sky relative to a subject, because you have neither an ultra-wide foreground trick nor a long lens — so you must choose a near object, change height, or use 2×.

**Name the five words.** If an assignment frame cannot take a five-word title, it is not ready to publish. Ask if you are unsure whether “empty” counts (it does, if emptiness is the idea).
