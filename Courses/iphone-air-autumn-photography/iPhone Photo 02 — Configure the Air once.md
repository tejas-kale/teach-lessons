HEIF, 24MP as the default, Grid and Level, Standard style — then stop touching Settings.

≈8 minutes · One evening, then leave it

The Camera app will fight you all month if formats reset, the grid is off, and Bright is cooking every leaf. This lesson is a single pass through Settings. After tonight, technique lives in Lessons 3–5, not in menus.

## Formats

Settings → Camera → Formats:

- **High Efficiency** (HEIF). Apple’s Photographic Styles on recent iPhones are part of the capture pipeline and can be changed later in Photos *if* the file is HEIF.[¹](#s1) Most Compatible (JPEG) throws that away.
- Turn on **Resolution Control** so the Camera app can show 24MP / 48MP.
- Leave the default at **24MP**. That is Apple’s Fusion still: detail plus HDR, and it still records depth so you can add a portrait blur later.[²](#s2)

Use *48MP HEIF* only for a static landscape you expect to crop. Night mode, Live Photos, flash, 2×, and Portrait typically will not give you a 48MP depth-rich file — independent Camera-app write-ups of the Air agree on that pattern; Apple does not print a mode-by-mode table.[³](#s3)

Settings → Camera → Preserve Settings: enable **Exposure Adjustment** and **Resolution Control** so the app does not silently reset.

> [!abstract] Project rule, not a spec
> GitHub Pages never gets the 48MP original. Export a JPEG at about 1600–2500 px on the long edge. Lesson 6 is the export ritual; do not invent a third resolution now.

## Composition aids and style

Settings → Camera: **Grid** and **Level** on. Apple documents both under setting up the shot.[⁴](#s4) The grid is for thirds and verticals (façades, trunks). The level is for horizons and canal edges.

Settings → Camera → Photographic Styles: start on **Standard**. Skip **Bright** for foliage — Apple describes it as brightening skin tones and applying a pop of vibrance.[¹](#s1) That is a people picture, not a linden.

Optional: Action button → Camera. Learn Camera Control on the lower-right edge: press to open or shoot; light double-press cycles Exposure / Zoom / Styles / Tone. Sliding changes the selected parameter without the jog of a pinch-zoom.[²](#s2)

## Check yourself

For a weekday neighbourhood walk, which capture setting should you use?

- [ ] 48MP HEIF on every walk, then crop later.
- [ ] JPEG capture so Photographic Styles stay editable.
- [ ] 24MP HEIF; 48MP only for still crops.
- [ ] ProRAW capture, then a light Photos edit.

> [!tip]- Reveal the answer
> 24MP HEIF is the Fusion default and keeps Styles-later plus depth-for-later. JPEG Most Compatible blocks restyling. The Air cannot shoot ProRAW. 48MP is the exception, not the walk.

## Focal lengths you already mapped

Customise Main Camera (wording varies by iOS) for 28 mm and 35 mm crops. They are composition discipline: they force a decision before the shutter. Combined with Lesson 1: 1× / 28 / 35 / 2×, and panorama when you cannot step back.

## Retrieval Practice

Why does this course default to HEIF rather than JPEG, in one sentence?

> [!example]- Model answer
> Because latest-generation Photographic Styles can be changed after capture only if the file is High Efficiency (HEIF). JPEG “Most Compatible” bakes the style in.

If you skipped Lesson 1’s four frames of the control tree, do them now with these settings locked.

**Ask if the menu names differ.** Resolution Control and Customise Main Camera have moved between iOS releases. Describe what you see; do not guess a ProRAW toggle into existence.
