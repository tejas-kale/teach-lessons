Reference

Lessons are read once. These are for coming back to.

  • Glossary — every term the course uses, defined once and used consistently.
  • Tuning dials — one page: every lever on an agent, what it costs, and when to reach for it.
  • Eval playbook — the harness, the metrics, and the failure taxonomy, on one printable sheet.

Workspace files

  • MISSION.md — why this course exists and what success looks like.
  • RESOURCES.md — the primary sources, ranked by trust, plus communities.
  • NOTES.md — teaching preferences and open threads.
  • learning-records/ — what has been learned, and what changed as a result.
These lessons are one half of the loop. The other half is asking your teacher about the runs that confuse you. Bring a failing trace, a benchmark number you don't trust, or a paper you can't place on the map.
